ZC-CORE is an open methodology. However, the FreeCuli brand, trademarks, and associated certification logos are strictly controlled to protect consumers and maintain the integrity of the standard. 

Open-source adoption of the ZC-CORE methodology does NOT automatically grant the right to use the "FreeCuli Certified" trademark.

## Conformance Claim Levels

To prevent false marketing and establish a clear governance hierarchy, ZC-CORE recognizes four distinct levels of methodology conformance:

### 1. ZC-CORE Self-Declared (Unverified)
*   **Definition:** A manufacturer publicly claims their device adheres to the M1-M10 invariants and follows the methodology.
*   **Trademark Usage:** The manufacturer MAY state "Designed based on ZC-CORE methodology principles" in text. They SHALL NOT use any FreeCuli logos or badges. FreeCuli assumes zero liability.

### 2. ZC-CORE Conformance Tested (Internal)
*   **Definition:** The manufacturer has internally executed the FC-ZC-CTS test specification and possesses internal laboratory evidence (e.g., oscilloscope traces) proving compliance.
*   **Trademark Usage:** The manufacturer MAY state "Tested to ZC-CORE Conformance Specifications." They SHALL NOT use the official certification badge.

### 3. Independently Verified (Third-Party)
*   **Definition:** An independent, accredited third-party hardware security laboratory has executed the FC-ZC-CTS tests on the production hardware and published a passing report.
*   **Trademark Usage:** The manufacturer MAY state "Independently Verified for ZC-CORE Compliance" and link to the lab report. 

### 4. FreeCuli Certified™ (Official)
*   **Definition:** FreeCuli has officially reviewed the Independent Verification reports, audited the compliance evidence, and issued a formal B2B Trademark License and Certification.
*   **Trademark Usage:** The manufacturer is granted full legal rights to place the **"FreeCuli Certified Zero-Cloud"** logo on product packaging, marketing materials, and digital interfaces.

## Commercial Exemption & B2B Licensing
Manufacturers utilizing FreeCuli's proprietary reference hardware designs (PCB schematics) must adhere to the CERN-OHL-S open-source obligations. To bypass these obligations and keep hardware designs proprietary while obtaining the official certification badge, manufacturers must acquire a Commercial Exemption and B2B Trademark License. Refer to `zero-cloud-dual-licensing.md`.
